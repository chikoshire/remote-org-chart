# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the at-a-glance view of Acme’s organisation.

## What you see

- **People** — active employment headcount
- **Departments** — how many teams appear in the data
- **Managers** — people with direct reports
- **Completeness** — share of profiles with title, department, and country filled in
- **Headcount by department / country** — visual breakdowns
- **Attention** — shortcuts into Data Health or incomplete People filters

## Refresh from Remote

Next to the synced timestamp, use **Refresh from Remote** to bypass the cache and rebuild the org snapshot. The timestamp means “last successful Remote pull.” While refreshing, the previous dashboard data stays on screen; if refresh fails, you keep that snapshot and see an error note.

## Jumping elsewhere

KPI cards and attention rows navigate into Org Chart, People, or Data Health.
